## What would flip a no

- A full spec pack (allergen sheet, shelf life after opening, compliant labels, invoicing that fits our purchasing system) plus a sample that tastes like something I can't make myself in the back bar.
- A price closer to 15 euros, plus written proof it keeps several weeks opened without the fridge and arrives with my usual distributor order.
- A technical sheet showing the alcohol level (under 0.5%), how long it keeps once opened, and a sample that tastes good enough to build a seasonal signature cocktail around it.
- A price close to 12-14 euros a litre with steady delivery guaranteed, and a tasting that shows me something I really can't make myself, that my customers ask for again.
- The sample tasting well in two or three of my own mocktails, plus the sugar per litre on paper, so I can show the owner it's a healthier base that sells at a premium.
- Three samples from three different batches that taste the same, plus a stated shelf life once opened, so I can trust it in a cocktail I sell every night.
- Tasting the sample and finding a flavour I genuinely can't make in-house, with a cost per cocktail low enough that the owner signs off at the quarterly review.
- If the sample shows a litre makes clearly more drinks than Monin (smaller dose per glass), so the cost per mocktail ends up close to what I pay now, I'd put one signature drink on the menu with it.
- Getting it through my usual distributor, with guaranteed stock all season and the same taste every batch, at a price closer to 15 euros a litre. I'd take the free sample to taste it, but that alone won't make me order.
- A price around 12 to 14 euros a litre, or a tasting that shows guests will actually pay more for a signature cocktail built on it.
- A price close to 12-14 euros a litre, or proof from a tasting that a signature drink with it sells at a higher price and my regulars actually order it.
- If my distributor carried it so it arrives with the rest of the stock, in a one-way bottle, at around 15 euros, I'd take the free sample and maybe try it on one signature drink.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
